Privacy Policy

App: MAN MCP Collaboration · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who uses this app

This app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google Drive (files and metadata), Google Sheets and Google Calendar, only to carry out tasks the owner asks it to perform.

How data is stored and shared

  • OAuth tokens and any data the app reads are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
